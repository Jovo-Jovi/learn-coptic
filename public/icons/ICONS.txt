PWA icons (icon-192.png, icon-512.png, icon-maskable-512.png),
docs/readme-hero.png, and public/og.png (WhatsApp / link preview)
are generated by scripts/render-brand-images.tsx.

Glyphs come from src/data/json/letters.json (Unicode) and are painted
with GNU FreeSerif (GPL-3.0-or-later WITH Font-exception-2.0). No
invented letters. Same Ⲁ as src/app/icon.tsx.
